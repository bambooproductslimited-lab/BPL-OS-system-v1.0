Privacy Policy

Last updated: 21 August 2026

Bamboo Products Limited ("BPL", "we", "us", or "our") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your rights, when you visit our website, contact us, or interact with our official social media accounts (Facebook, Instagram, TikTok, and WhatsApp Business).

1. Information we collect

We may collect the following types of information:

  • Information you provide directly — such as your name, phone number, email address, and message content when you contact us through our website, WhatsApp Business, or by commenting or messaging us on Facebook, Instagram, or TikTok.
  • Website usage information — such as pages visited, time on site, and general location, collected through analytics tools (e.g. Google Analytics) to help us understand how our website is used.
  • Public social media interactions — such as comments, likes, and follows on our official social media pages, as made available to us by those platforms in accordance with their own privacy policies.

2. How we use your information

  • To respond to inquiries, quotation requests, and customer support questions;
  • To process and fulfil orders and communicate about deliveries;
  • To manage and improve our social media presence, including replying to comments and messages;
  • To understand how visitors use our website, so we can improve it;
  • To comply with our legal obligations.

3. Third-party platforms

When you interact with us via Facebook, Instagram, TikTok, or WhatsApp Business, that platform's own privacy policy also applies to how it handles your information. We access data from these platforms (such as public comments and messages sent to our official accounts) only to the extent needed to respond to you and to measure the performance of our own posts and pages. We do not sell or share this information with third parties for their own marketing purposes.

4. Cookies and analytics

Our website may use cookies and similar technologies, including Google Analytics, to understand website traffic and usage patterns. You can control cookies through your browser settings; disabling cookies may affect some website functionality.

5. Data retention

We retain personal information only for as long as necessary to fulfil the purposes described in this Policy, or as required by law.

6. Your rights

You may ask us to access, correct, or delete personal information we hold about you by contacting us using the details below.

7. Data security

We take reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect the information we hold from unauthorised access, loss, or misuse.

8. Changes to this Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.
